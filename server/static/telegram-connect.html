Connect your Telegram

Dana reads your own chats and channels to list and summarize what's unread. It never sends messages and never marks anything as read.

  1. Open Telegram on your phone.
  2. Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device.
  3. Scan this code.

The code renews by itself until you scan it.

Your account has two-step verification. Enter its password to finish.

Connected.

To remove access later, disconnect Telegram on the Integrations page and remove "Dana" from Telegram's Devices list.
