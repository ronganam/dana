    Why it's different

    Not another chat box with access to your email.

    Chat assistants wait for you to type. Dana lives in what's happening around you: your notifications, your calls, the car, the train, the bank. It acts on what it sees, and it can undo anything.

    What it does

    One assistant, wired into the whole house.

    Now

    One queue for everything that needs you.

    Approvals, reminders, messages worth answering, a train to catch, a payment that looks wrong. Every add-on adds its own cards, and each card takes one tap.

    Approve: reply to Gal Passport renewal · due Fri 08:12 · platform 3 Card charged twice?

    Notifications

    Sorted, explained, escalated.

    Noise folds away, and the urgent ones keep at you until you see them. Dana also suggests rules from what you keep ignoring.

    Calls

    Every call becomes the next step.

    Recordings are transcribed and turned into tasks and events. When someone you know calls, you see who they are and what's open with them.

    Triggers

    Time, place, car, waking up.

    Reminders that wait for the moment you can act: leaving work, getting in the car, getting out of bed.

    People

    A short brief on everyone.

    Who they are, what you last talked about, what you promised them. It's ready before a meeting or a call.

    Money

    Your banks and cards, explained.

    A twice-daily sync of your accounts, with spending split into fixed, yearly, everyday and one-off. You get a balance forecast, ways to save that Dana checks back on, and your investments and meal card, read-only.

    Your accounts

    Gmail, WhatsApp, Calendar, Contacts, Telegram.

    Read, search and summarize; write email and WhatsApp messages. Nothing goes out without asking you first. Text written by other people can never make Dana act on its own.

    Home

    Home Assistant and cameras.

    Lights, heating and locks, room by room, by voice or text. Camera clips and live views on the TV and the dashboard.

    Projects

    Decisions, side by side.

    Renovating? Buying a car? Options in columns, with notes, files, links, a budget and a date, all searchable.

    Safe by default

    It acts first, and can always undo.

    Things you can take back happen right away, with Undo. Sending something in your name, or deleting something for good, waits for your yes. If an email or a message tells Dana to do something, it ignores it.

    Make it yours

    Your server. Your add-ons.

    Dana runs on one small server you own: a mini PC, a NAS or a homelab. Each feature is an add-on with its own tools and screens. The train add-on in the showreel lives in its own repository and installs from the catalog. You can write yours in an afternoon: an add-on is just typed Python functions with docstrings, and Dana takes it from there.

    • Any model through LiteLLM. Claude is the default.
    • Several users on one server, each with their own accounts, data and tokens.
    • Docker and SQLite. Nothing to configure: open the setup link and paste one model key.
    • MIT licensed. Your data stays on your server.

    What it costs

    About $15 a month in model calls.

    That's what one real household's Dana spends, with every call to a model counted: chats, every notification read and sorted, call recordings, briefings. Measured over eight full days in November 2026 on the default models (Claude for chat, Gemini Flash-Lite for the background work). You pay your model provider directly; the server is yours, so there's no subscription. Set a monthly budget and Dana warns you at 80% and pauses its background work at 100%.
