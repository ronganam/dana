    How it works

    Everything in. One thing out.

    Dana reads what happens around you on your own server, sorts it, understands it, remembers what matters and decides when to tell you. Most of it, you never hear about.

    • In: your phone's notifications, call recordings, your phone's signals (waking up, the car, saved places, sleep), your accounts (Gmail, Calendar, WhatsApp, Contacts), your bank and cards through an add-on, and you (chat, voice, the watch).
    • On your server, each one is sorted (needs you, can wait, noise), understood (calls transcribed, dates, promises and people pulled out), remembered (facts about you, briefs on people) and Dana decides when and where to tell you.
    • Out: the Now queue, your watch, the wall tablet and TV, or a push to your phone. Most things are folded away and never reach you.
    • The model provider gets only the text a step needs, one call at a time, with your own key.

    Make it yours

    Your server. Your add-ons.

    Dana runs on one small server you own: a mini PC, a NAS or a homelab. Each feature is an add-on with its own tools and screens. The train add-on in the showreel lives in its own repository and installs from the catalog. You can write yours in an afternoon: an add-on is just typed Python functions with docstrings, and Dana takes it from there.

    • Any model through LiteLLM. Claude is the default.
    • Several users on one server, each with their own accounts, data and tokens.
    • Docker and SQLite. Nothing to configure: open the setup link and paste one model key.
    • MIT licensed. Your data stays on your server.

    What it costs

    About $15 a month in model calls.

    • 49%Chat with Dana
    • 32%Reading and sorting notifications
    • 8%Transcribing and reading calls
    • 5%People briefs
    • 5%Money
    • 1%Everything else: briefings, voice notes, learning about you

    That's what one real household's Dana spends, with every call to a model counted: chats, every notification read and sorted, call recordings, briefings. Measured over eight full days in November 2026 on the default models (Claude for chat, Gemini Flash-Lite for the background work). You pay your model provider directly; the server is yours, so there's no subscription. Set a monthly budget and Dana warns you at 80% and pauses its background work at 100%.
